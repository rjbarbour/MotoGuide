## Length And Diversity Iterations

Iteration 1 changed the prompt to explicit word ranges:

- Short Facts target: 35-50 words.
- Long Facts target: 80-100 words.
- Diverse fixture added nations, counties, towns, and POI-like rows.
- Result: Short Facts averaged 45-48 words depending on fixture; Long Facts averaged about 93-94 words.
- Failure modes: some short rows drifted just over range, nation rows still used schoolbook definitions, Stroud drifted to "Slad Valley", and Severn Bridge was sometimes treated as surrounding townscape rather than the bridge.

Iteration 2 tightened length and national-definition wording:

- Short Facts target: 35-45 words.
- Long Facts target: 75-90 words.
- Result on diverse fixture: Short Facts averaged 42.2 words; Long Facts averaged 83.6 words.
- Failure modes: England long fact still began with constitutional definition; Stroud still drifted to "Slad Valley"; Severn Bridge phrasing still treated the bridge as an area.

Iteration 3 added POI-specific handling and stronger false-precision guardrails:

- Prompt says POIs such as cathedral, abbey, common, bridge, castle, museum, pass, or viewpoint should be described as objects or sites, not surrounding settlements.
- Prompt says not to place towns inside a named sub-valley or neighbourhood unless that exact sub-place is in the request.
- Prompt says nations and countries should avoid constitutional status, size, or UK membership definitions.
- Result on diverse fixture: Short Facts averaged 44.8 words; Long Facts averaged 82.9 words among successful rows.
- Result on Gloucestershire fixture: Short Facts averaged 41.6 words; Long Facts averaged 85.0 words.
- Residual issue: model still sometimes misses exact ranges by a few words. Keep this prompt-controlled for now; adding a hard word cap should be done only when server and iOS sanitizers are aligned.

Source spot-check notes:

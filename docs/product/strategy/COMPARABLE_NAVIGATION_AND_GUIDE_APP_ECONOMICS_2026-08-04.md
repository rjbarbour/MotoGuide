# Comparable navigation and guide-app economics

**Date:** 2026-08-04
**Purpose:** Pricing, scale and economic signals relevant to RideHorizon. All prices are current storefront prices unless marked historical.

## Bottom line

- Specialist motorcycle navigation subscriptions range from roughly €15 to US$80 a year. The main UK cluster is £40–£50: Scenic £49.90, Detecht £49.99, REVER US$39.99 and calimoto US$79.99. RideHorizon's proposed £39.99 annual price is credible but will need to earn renewal despite offering a narrower job than full navigation.
- The best public paid-conversion datapoint is Roadpass: 119,000 paid memberships from 6 million registered users in 2022, or 1.98%. This covered a multi-app travel ecosystem, not one app, but it supports using 1–2% of broad registrations as a conservative planning range until RideHorizon has its own cohorts.
- Guide products use materially different models. GuideAlong sells lifetime destination packs, typically US$15–US$25 and up to US$150 for bundles. VoiceMap shares roughly half of post-store proceeds with creators. Autio combines a US$35.99 annual subscription with a US$29.99 non-renewing 30-day pass.
- No comparable publishes dependable CAC, churn, subscriber count or a standalone subscription P&L. Those inputs must remain explicit RideHorizon assumptions, not claimed market benchmarks.
- REVER's acquisition by Comoto and THOR's US$81 million proposed sale of a controlling interest in the whole Roadpass Digital portfolio show strategic value to adjacent ecosystem owners. Neither proves standalone app profitability.

## Scale sensitivity, not revenue estimates

The table applies 1%, 2% and 5% annual paid conversion to cumulative public user/download claims. It is deliberately mechanical: cumulative users are not active users, annual subscribers or unique purchasers.

| Product and public scale base | 1% paid | 2% paid | 5% paid |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| calimoto: 1 million users × US$79.99/year | US$0.80m | US$1.60m | US$4.00m |
| REVER: 1 million downloads × US$39.99/year | US$0.40m | US$0.80m | US$2.00m |
| Autio: [900,000 installs](https://autio.com/here-and-there/yellowstone-road-trip-guide-2026) × [US$35.99/year](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/autio-road-trip-travel-app/id1300494609) | US$0.32m | US$0.65m | US$1.62m |

These figures are retail billings before tax, refunds, store commission, discounts and operating costs. Apple normally pays 70% in a subscriber's first year and 85% thereafter; eligible developers in the [App Store Small Business Program](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/small-business-program/) receive 85% from day one.

## Implications for RideHorizon

1. **Keep £39.99/year as the base case.** It sits below UK motorcycle-navigation leaders and near REVER and Beeline Plus. The value case should be framed around automatic geographic awareness, not parity with full navigation suites.
2. **Test the touring pass rather than locking £9.99.** It is inexpensive beside GuideAlong's US$15–US$25 single-destination tours and Autio's US$29.99 30-day pass. Test £9.99 as an acquisition offer against £14.99 as the standard price.
3. **Use 1%, 2% and 5% conversion scenarios.** Treat 1–2% as the cold, broad-audience range and 5% as an engaged or qualified-audience case. Do not infer conversion from App Store ratings.
4. **Retain the current unit-economics calculation as an assumption.** At £39.99 including 20% VAT and a 15% store commission, proceeds are £28.33. With £4.50 marginal service/content cost, annual contribution is £23.83. At £18 CAC and 35% annual churn, contribution CLV is £68.08 and CLV:CAC is 3.78×. Competitor disclosures do not validate the £18 CAC, £4.50 cost or 35% churn inputs.
5. **Measure renewal before scaling paid acquisition.** The crucial unknown is whether a passive awareness product becomes habitual enough to renew. Report channel CAC, trial-to-paid conversion, first renewal, second renewal and annual contribution by acquisition cohort.
6. **Keep content costs structurally low.** GuideAlong and Autio demonstrate front-loaded editorial production with low probable delivery cost; VoiceMap demonstrates the alternative of externalising production while surrendering roughly half of post-fee sales. RideHorizon's on-device and cached-content design is economically preferable if quality can be maintained.

## Caveats

- Storefront prices vary by country, tax treatment, platform, promotion and legacy plan.
- User, download, trip and route counts are mostly cumulative marketing claims, not monthly active users.
- Roadpass conversion covers multiple apps, memberships and bundles in 2022; it is directional, not a direct RideHorizon benchmark.
- Public acquisitions and funding rounds reveal strategic interest and capital requirements, not profit.
- No dependable public competitor CAC, churn, gross margin, subscriber count or cohort-retention data was found. Any model using those inputs is a RideHorizon scenario, not an observed market fact.

RideHorizon

An iPhone audio place-awareness companion for motorcyclists, designed to work alongside normal navigation.

Private beta
RideHorizon is currently being tested with a small group of invited riders. It is not navigation, safety or emergency software.

Hear the places around you

RideHorizon can announce towns, counties and regions through the iPhone speaker or a Bluetooth helmet headset. Riders can choose place names, optional short or long AI facts, Apple Voice or Premium Voice.

Built to stay in the background

Start a ride while stopped, keep your normal navigation app in charge, and end the ride when finished. RideHorizon is designed to minimise interaction while moving.
